This agreement lets a company, university or other organization (the
"Entity") authorize its employees or contractors to contribute to Assetarium
(the "Project"), maintained by Bruno Cesar Puli Dala Rosa ("we" or "us"). The
Entity keeps the copyright in its Contributions and grants us a license to
use them. Each authorized person should also sign the
[Individual CLA](CLA.md) (or the signature mechanism in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)) so that their pull requests can be
matched to this agreement.

## 1. Definitions

- **"You"** or **"Entity"** means the legal entity signing this agreement,
  together with all entities that control, are controlled by, or are under
  common control with it. "Control" means direct or indirect ownership of
  more than 50% of the voting securities or the power to direct its
  management.
- **"Designated Contributors"** means the employees and contractors listed in
  Schedule A, as updated under section 9.
- **"Contribution"** means any work of authorship, including code,
  documentation, images, 3D assets, configuration, tests and any
  modifications or additions to existing work, that a Designated Contributor
  or the Entity intentionally submits to us for inclusion in the Project, by
  any means. Communication clearly marked in writing as "Not a Contribution"
  is excluded.
- **"Submit"** means to send any form of electronic, verbal or written
  communication to us, including through code review and issue tracking
  systems managed by or for the Project.
